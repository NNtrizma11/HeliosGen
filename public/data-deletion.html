Data Deletion Instructions

HeliosGen, operated by Hermes Global OOD, Sofia, Bulgaria

HeliosGen only stores advertising data from the Meta ad accounts a business chose to connect (see our Privacy Policy). You can remove it in two ways.

1. Revoke HeliosGen's access

  1. Open Meta Business Settings for the business you connected.
  2. Go to Integrations → Connected apps (or Business integrations).
  3. Select HeliosGen and click Remove.

HeliosGen can no longer read any data from your ad accounts once access is removed.

2. Delete the data we already hold

Email info@nutrizma.com with the subject "HeliosGen data deletion" and include the business name or ad account IDs. We will delete the access token and all synced ad, creative and performance data for those accounts within 30 days and confirm by email.
